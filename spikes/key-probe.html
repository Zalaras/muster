How to run this
  1. Open at least three tabs in this window before starting, so a ⌘-digit tab switch is actually observable.
  2. Press the combo shown. If the page records it, it advances by itself.
  3. If the browser stole it instead (new window, tab switch, nothing at all), click Browser took it.
  4. If a new window or tab opened, close it and come back by clicking the window/tab — do not press another ⌘-digit to get back, or you will spend the next candidate on it.
  5. At the end, copy the results block and paste it back to Claude.
