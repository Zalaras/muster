C · Stacks + picker dialog ALTERNATIVE

A collapsed group reads as a stack of cards (a titled card with a shadowed edge), not a thin section rule; expanded, its members hang off an indented line. The summary is a proportional state bar plus explicit counts only for needs-input and failed. Selection is Gmail-style: a checkbox appears on hover at a card's corner, ticking one brings up the bar. Moving is a picker dialog ("Move 2 sessions to…" with member counts and New group…) rather than a drop-down. Filter is per-section: a hide button on hover hides a section, the ⌸ in the rail head lists what is hidden. Attention sort flattens the rail — one list, each card wearing its group chip — and manual puts the stacks back. "Everything else" is called Unfiled.

Try: click PR REVIEWS to collapse it into a stack · hover a card, tick the box, then Move to ▾ for the picker · + group opens a name dialog · hover a header and press hide, then ⌸ to bring it back · sort: attention flattens into one list with chips · header ⋯ → Delete group… shows the three outcomes.
