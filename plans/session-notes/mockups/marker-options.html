Session notes: the rail card and tile header marker

The marker shows only while the note has text. It is the header's notebook glyph at 11px in the muted foreground. Hovering it opens a read-only panel with the note rendered, cut off after 12 lines with a fade (the same 350 ms delay, follow and close rules as the cache timer's panel). Nothing on the card or tile edits the note. Each option pairs a rail-card placement with the tile placement that matches it. The bottom row shows the hover open on a long note.
