Session notes: where the Focus header's note glyph goes

The same notebook glyph in four places. It is muted while the note is empty and in the foreground colour once the note has text. Clicking it opens the note panel anchored under it; the panel's top-right control pops it out (see panel.html). Each option is shown with no note, with a note, with the panel open, with a long title, and at a 640px header. The rail and tile marker is a separate choice (marker-options.html).
