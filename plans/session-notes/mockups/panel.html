Session notes: the note panel

One panel in two forms. Anchored under the header glyph (option A): click-away, the glyph or Escape closes it. Popped out: floats over the main slot, never over the rail, masthead or Focus header, and only its close button (×), pop-in (⧠) or Escape with focus inside it close it, so you can type in the terminal with it open. The header always names the session the note belongs to. One click on the rendered note edits the raw text, and leaving edit mode (Escape, or a click outside the text) renders it again. Saving is automatic and says nothing while it works. The header reads unsaved only while a save can't reach musterd.
