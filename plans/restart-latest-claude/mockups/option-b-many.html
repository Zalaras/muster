Option B with many sessions

The version readout is the control: claude 2.1.317→318 with a dot, in the readout's fixed width. These are three ways its panel can cope with 14 live sessions. Each is shown all idle, all busy, mixed and queued (after confirming, while 5 wait their turn; there is no Cancel). "Idle" means idle with no background tasks, or failed: the sessions that restart at once. The readout label always shows the oldest version a live session runs, so 2.1.316 wins over 2.1.317. While sessions are queued it reads claude ↻5 queued. Once nothing is behind, it goes back to claude 2.1.318 with no dot.
