the section header's caret button, `.ghead .disc` (`web/src/render/railsections.ts:161`).

U+25BE `▾` is the *small* triangle. It also comes from a fallback font, since the computed family is
Arial, which has no such glyph. It grows slowly: even 20px gives only a 7 px stem. U+25BC `▼` at 12px
already gives 8×8.

## Holds

Holds as written for visibility: a 3×6 px mark is hard to see. The "use" part holds with a narrower
scope. The click target is already more than the glyph. The 16×16 button folds the section, and so
does any click on the header's free space and summary (`railsections.ts:186-191`,
kb:adr/rail-group-name-click-does-not-fold). Only a renameable group's name (by that ADR), the
select-mode checkbox and `⋯` do not fold. The hit box does not need to become the whole row, because
it nearly is already, and making the name fold would re-open an accepted ADR. The fix is the glyph's
drawn size, plus a modestly larger button.

## Cause

`web/src/style.css:4031-4051`: `.ghead .disc` sets `font-size: 10px` and draws `content: "▾"` (U+25BE
SMALL down-pointing triangle) in `::before`. That glyph's ink is about 40–60 % of the em in both
directions, and it comes from a system fallback font, so 10px renders as a 3×6 / 6×4 px mark. The
mockup `docs/design/mockups/groups/shared.css:93-95` has the same values, so the code is faithful to a
mockup that drew it too small. No size is specified anywhere else: the groups spec and design-system
§ Section header both say only "caret button".

## Proof

- Level: render (kb:adr/process-test-levels-journey-property-logic). This is what the display shows for
  a given snapshot. No daemon round trip is needed, because fixture `groups-titled-collapsed` already
  has one collapsed section (Alpha) and one expanded section (Bravo).
- File: `web/render/groups.spec.ts`. Written by **render-tests**.
- Name: `a section header's caret is drawn large enough to see, collapsed and expanded (#113)`.
- Assertions:
  1. For Alpha (collapsed) and Bravo (expanded), `inkBox(groupCaret(head))` has a long side of at least
     8 px and a short side of at least 5 px. Red today: collapsed `3x6`, expanded `6x4`.
  2. `groupCaret(head)`'s bounding box is at least 20×20. Red today: `16x16`.
  3. Guard: the collapsed ink is taller than it is wide and the expanded ink is wider than it is tall, so
     the arrow still points right or down.

  The thresholds go with the developer's chosen option. The ones above fit option A. For option B, use
  long ≥ 8 and short ≥ 8; for option C, long ≥ 9 and short ≥ 5.

## Route

`fix`. This is a CSS change to an existing surface, with no protocol, spec requirement or new UI surface.

## Questions

1. **How big should the caret be drawn, and how?** Each option below has a 20×20 button and the row
   fold unchanged. A is my default if the developer just says "go".
   - **A (recommended):** a CSS-drawn solid triangle on `::before` (`clip-path` on a `currentColor`
     box), 9 px wide × 6 px tall expanded and 6×9 rotated when collapsed, in `--fg-muted`, with `--fg`
     on header hover. It does not depend on a fallback font and is about 1.5× today's long side and 2×
     its stem. It reads at the weight of the name's caps.
   - **B:** keep a font glyph but swap to `▼` (U+25BC) at 12px, measured at 8×8. This is the smallest
     diff, but the glyph still comes from a fallback font.
   - **C:** keep `▾` and raise it to 16px, measured at 5×9. This is the most conservative change, and it
     is still a thin arrow.
2. Hit box: is it fine to leave the whole-row fold as it is, with the name of a renameable group
   excluded per kb:adr/rail-group-name-click-does-not-fold? I recommend yes. Making the name fold too
   would supersede that ADR, which is not a small-track change.

## Answers

Developer, 2026-11-03, verbatim: "5. A"

Option A: CSS-drawn triangle (no font glyph), ~9×6 px expanded / 6×9 px collapsed, in --fg-muted with --fg on hover, button 20×20. Row click behaviour unchanged; the group name stays non-folding (kb:adr/rail-group-name-click-does-not-fold stands).
