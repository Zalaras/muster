#34 · Which idle sessions have I read?

Each rail has three idle cards: two unread (they went idle while you were elsewhere) and one read (you focused it after it went idle). The focused card is always read by definition. A working card is included to show that the marker only applies to idle. Design rule in force: state colour means state and nothing else, so every marker here is neutral (--fg, --fg-muted, --edge) and none uses amber.
