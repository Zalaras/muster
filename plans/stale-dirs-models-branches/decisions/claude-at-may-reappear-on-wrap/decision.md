# Decision: claude-at-may-reappear-on-wrap

**Outcome**: A — accept it. The Focus header's `↳` block may hide and then show again as the window narrows, whenever the header gains a row; the rail card and the tile glyph always show the move.
**Reached by**: user decision (the developer, 2026-10-01, review cycle 4 browser Minor 3)
**Decisive argument**: The move is always visible on the rail card and the tile header; the Focus header's `↳` block is a bonus readout, so its showing wherever it fits is preferred over hiding it where it would fit.
**Dissent to honour**: Option B (once hidden, stay hidden at every narrower width) was not chosen.
**Landed in**: `docs/adr/focus-mainhead-wraps-to-second-row-when-narrow.md` (proposed, Consequences)
